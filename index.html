How we get these numbers

App ratings and versions come from the apps' US App Store listings (snapshot 12 November 2026). Drama titles, episode counts and view counts come from the platforms' public catalogs (snapshot 12 November 2026). Coin and subscription prices come from the apps' published price pages and store listings (snapshot 2026-11-12). Where a price is not officially published — DramaBox coin costs — we show a clearly labelled estimate range instead of a single made-up number. More about our data.

DramaFinish is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with ReelShort, DramaBox or any other app listed here. Prices and ratings are snapshots (apps: 12 November 2026; pricing: 2026-11-12) and change over time — always check the app before paying.
