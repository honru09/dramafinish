The hook

Althea, Atlantis’ heiress, is killed by her jealous reborn half-sister Mara at her wedding. Reborn three days early, she finds Mara’s affair with her fiancé and marries vagabond Dorian. Dorian is Poseidon, the sea god, who comes to repay a debt to her mother. Mara throws Althea into the sea, yet Poseidon reveals his true form. With the tide necklace, Althea defeats Mara and becomes Queen of Atlantis.
