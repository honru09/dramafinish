The hook

Elisa Carter is half human, half vampire — an orphan taken in by the powerful Lockhart family. For ten years she loved and cared for the Lockhart triplets, the brothers who swore to protect her. She even planned to give up her vampire side, become fully human, and marry into the family that raised her.

Then Amber, the maid's daughter, gets to work — and the triplets believe her lies over the girl they grew up with. Elisa is framed, humiliated and abandoned by the three people she trusted most, her dignity stripped away in front of the school that once adored her.

So on her eighteenth birthday, Elisa makes the opposite choice: night school, full vampire, gone. And waiting in her new life is Caine Trueblood, an elite vampire lord — just as the triplets begin to realise what they threw away. The only question left is whether their regret will be worth anything at all.

The ending — tap to reveal (major spoilers)

Elisa makes her choice: she transfers to night school and becomes a full vampire. Caine Trueblood — the vampire lord she was promised to by an old family pact — takes her in, and she builds a new life at his side. Amber's lies unravel: the maid's daughter who framed Elisa is exposed as the manipulator she always was. The triplets finally see what they did and come back begging for forgiveness.
