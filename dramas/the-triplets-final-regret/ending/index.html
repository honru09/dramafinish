The ending

Elisa makes her choice: she transfers to night school and becomes a full vampire. Caine Trueblood — the vampire lord she was promised to by an old family pact — takes her in, and she builds a new life at his side. Amber's lies unravel: the maid's daughter who framed Elisa is exposed as the manipulator she always was. The triplets finally see what they did and come back begging for forgiveness. Jacob, Oliver and Nash get their regret — but they do not get Elisa. She never goes back. The brothers who swore to protect her end up watching her walk away for good.
