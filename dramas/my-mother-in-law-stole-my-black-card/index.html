The hook

During her three-year marriage to Ethan, Claire hides her identity as the Bennett Group heiress while enduring slights. After learning that her mother-in-law, Linda, stole her black card to fund Ethan’s mistress, Sophia, and celebrate Sophia’s pregnancy, Claire enables purchase verification. Linda’s charges near two million dollars before payment is blocked, and Claire coldly refuses to provide the verification code.
