The hook

On the eve of her wedding, Teyo discovers the unthinkable: her own sister, Malin, has seduced her fiancé, Zuma. It wasn't love. Malin did it to escape her own fate — she was the one meant to be sacrificed to Huitzil, the feared God of War.

Teyo refuses to marry a man who betrayed her on the night before their wedding. She prays for mercy for her people — and gets the god's attention instead. Huitzil no longer wants Malin. He wants Teyo, and he is determined to claim her soul.

Prepared to die for her empire, Teyo stands before the god — and the unpredictable Huitzil spares her, carrying her off to his realm. But Malin's lies are still spreading back home, and Zuma's regret is coming for them both.

The ending — tap to reveal (major spoilers)

Teyo takes her sister's place on the altar — and Huitzil does the thing no god had done before: instead of taking her life, he intervenes in the ritual and carries her to his realm, bending the rules of the sacrifice and compromising his own authority for her. Malin is exposed as a dark sorceress, and Zuma, drowning in remorse, fights to win Teyo back. She forgives him — for her own peace, so the betrayal stops defining her — but she does not choose him. In the final episode, Teyo chooses Huitzil, and the two of them get the quiet, happy ending neither was supposed to have.
