Serena Shelton, the eldest daughter of the Shelton family, lost her mother at a young age. After her mother’s passing, her father’s mistress took over. In a bid to reclaim the Shelton Group, founded by her mother, Serena reluctantly agrees to marry Cyril Hawkins, the eldest son of the wealthiest family, in place of her half-sister, Chloe. A year ago, Cyril was attacked and left mentally impaired.
