Queen Mom Rules — how it ends

Our Payoff score for this ending: 8/10. The cliffhangers pay out — this one lands.

The ending

Athena stops waiting for her family to change. Once the truth about who she and her sons really are is confirmed in front of the whole family, the mother and siblings who called her low-class and barred her from the table fall to their knees. It changes nothing. Athena has finally seen that no amount of patience would fix people who only value money and status, so she does the thing she never did before: she chooses herself. She severs ties with the family that humiliated her and walks away with her sons — and when the same greed shows up again closer to home, she shuts it down just as fast.
