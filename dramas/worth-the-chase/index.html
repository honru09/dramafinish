The hook

Ryan Collins has loved her best friend since childhood. Carsen Blake is the school's football prodigy — kind, golden, and completely out of her league — until one unexpected night he is hers, and the next day he confesses he feels the same.

Three weeks later, a positive pregnancy test lands in Ryan's hands — days before Carsen leaves for the NFL tryouts he has dreamed about since he was a kid. Ryan makes the decision for both of them: she will not be the girl who costs him his future.

So she kisses another man, right in front of him, and lets him leave believing the worst. What Carsen doesn't know is that he is leaving two people behind — and Ryan intends to keep it that way.

The ending — tap to reveal (major spoilers)

Carsen comes back a year later as a college football star — and chooses Ryan before he knows the truth, offering to raise her baby even while he believes the child is another man's.
