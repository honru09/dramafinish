The hook

Six years ago, Anita spent one night with a stranger for money, said goodbye to this cold city, and disappeared abroad. She came back with a son — and a past she intended to keep buried.

Then fate does its work. Her new job puts her under Tristan, the legendary CEO — and her little boy takes one look at her handsome new boss and starts calling him 'dad'.

Anita knows what that night was, and she knows who her son is. The only person who doesn't is the man signing her paychecks — and every day at that office, the secret gets harder to keep.
