The hook

Lyra, a human, stumbles into the path of Kael, the Dragon King — and one passionate night changes both of their lives. Then a prophecy lands between them: a fake one, though Kael doesn't know that. Believing she will betray him, he casts her out.

What he never learns is that Lyra leaves carrying his child. Amber is born half-dragon and raised hidden away — until the little girl falls sick with something no human healer can fix.

So Lyra does the one thing she swore she would never do: she walks back into the dragon realm to beg help from the king who threw her away — while jealous rivals circle, and the daughter he never knew existed stands between them.

The ending — tap to reveal (major spoilers)

In the final confrontation, Kael reaches Lyra and Amber in time and defeats the rival who faked the prophecy — the lie that made him cast Lyra out is exposed in front of everyone. Amber survives her first full dragon transformation, and Kael finally accepts her openly as his daughter, the first dragonborn in a thousand years. Lyra does not forgive him instantly; he earns it by protecting them both. The series closes with the family standing together as the realm recognises Lyra and Kael as Dragon King and Dragon Queen — the woman he banished returns as his queen, with their daughter between them.
