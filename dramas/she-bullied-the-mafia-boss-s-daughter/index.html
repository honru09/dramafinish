Synopsis (from the platform catalog)

Sofia, sole heir to a Sicily mafia don, hid in Zurich for 15 years. Returning for her father's wedding, she is mistaken for his mistress by Maria, his scheming bride-to-be, and beaten nearly to death. Framed and dumped, Sofia finally fights back. At the gala she drops her disguise, exposes Maria's fake crash, and claims the family throne.
