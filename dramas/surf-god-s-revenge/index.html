The hook

Duke Koa is the God of the Island — a compassionate god whose greatest treasure is his surfboard. Then the Sea Hag curses him and shrinks the board, and names her price: marry her human daughter, Kai, and stay with her for five years. Help Kai succeed, and his life returns to normal.

Duke keeps his side of the bargain completely. He becomes a devoted househusband, builds Kai's career from nothing, and absorbs her contempt — she sees him as an opportunistic, worthless husband, and she says so. He tells himself he can endure anything until the five years are over.

Then Kai's ex works his way back into her life, and something in the god finally gives way. Five years of mistreatment are about to come due — and Kai has no idea who she has been married to.

The ending — tap to reveal (major spoilers)

When the five years run out, Duke stops enduring. At the QuickMaple expo he reveals what he has hidden for the whole marriage: he rides the waves as the God of the Island, eyes glowing, power on full display — in front of Kai, her ex Chad, and everyone who treated him as a worthless househusband. Kai, who signed the divorce papers certain he would come crawling back, runs after him begging for forgiveness and promising to change. Duke's answer is a smirk and a turned back. He leaves with Angela Lee — the woman who defended his dignity long before she knew what he was — and returns to the ocean and his own life. Kai is left with the echoes of her own pleas.
