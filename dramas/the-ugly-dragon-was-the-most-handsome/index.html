Synopsis (from the platform catalog)

Eli Valen was called ugly since birth, hidden behind a silver mask and sent to his grandmother in the forest. At eighteen he returns to the family estate to claim the crest for the dragon trial, and is humiliated again. When his mask shatters before the whole court and his sisters find the letters he sent, the family learns what they threw away.
