Synopsis (from the platform catalog)

Framed for killing her abusive husband, Selina works as a servant at the Black Estate to reclaim her deaf daughter Lily. Her husband's older brother Alexander blamed her without proof and used Lily as leverage, yet as they hunt the real killer and fight for custody, hate turns into forbidden love. Together they clear her name and build a family.
