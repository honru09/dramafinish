Synopsis (from the platform catalog)

Wayne, the last adult Dragon God, hides his power to raise his daughter Rhea as an ordinary "lizard" dad. When snobbish nobles Dario and Vivian and their bullying child torment Rhea at an elite magic academy, Wayne's secret begins to unravel. Isabelle, a White Tiger Queen who loves him, stands by his side as the truth comes to light. Their story culminates in a ring and Wayne's heartfelt proposal.
