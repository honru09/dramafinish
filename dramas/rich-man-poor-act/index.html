The hook

Ella, an undercover NYPD officer, mistakes Liam, a wealthy tycoon, for her contact while on a mission. By a twist of fate, they end up getting married. To fulfill his grandfather’s wish, the two enter a contract marriage. Liam hides his identity as the CEO and pretends to be unemployed. Yet as he protects Ella from her greedy relatives, he falls for her. When his ex-girlfriend Isla returns with lies and his secret is exposed in public, a heartbroken Ella files for divorce, and Liam sets out to win her back.
