The hook

Fifteen years ago, a marriage pact was signed between mafia families: Alice Castro will marry Pietro, the notorious mob boss. Her brother Augusto has watched that deadline approach like an execution date.

So Augusto does something insane. He exploits the pact's own wording — a child of the Castro family must marry the boss — and steps in to marry Pietro in his sister's place. The mafia world is scandalised. His family calls it madness.

What Augusto doesn't know is that none of this is his idea at all. The marriage was a love trap, set by Pietro himself — and Augusto has just walked into it wearing a wedding suit.
