The hook

Adam Hawke is the world's greatest secret agent. His newest assignment is nothing like the others: Raymond Strauss, the most dangerous man alive, is on the move — and Adam's cover is the last place anyone would look for him. He is going undercover as a nanny.

The child he is protecting is eight years old. She is also, though she doesn't know it, his daughter. Adam has to guard her, earn her trust, and win over her mother — all without ever revealing who he really is.

But a double life has a price. Every mission pulls him back into the open, every lie brings Strauss closer, and one wrong move could destroy the family he has only just found.
