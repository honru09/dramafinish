Synopsis (from the platform catalog)

After her parents cause a drunk-driving accident that kills his family, mafia boss Dominic imprisons and tortures his innocent fiancée, Elena, for five years. When she finally throws herself into the river, he realizes too late that he loves her. But his regret comes too late. Some wounds never heal.
