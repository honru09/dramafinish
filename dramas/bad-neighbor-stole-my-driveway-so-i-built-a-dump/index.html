The hook

Faced with her powerful and malicious neighbor, Emily chose not to resort to violence. Instead, she bided her time and devised a plan. For her daughter, who fell into a coma after the emergency access was blocked by luxury cars; for her grandfather, who died of rage; for her humiliated father; and for the sole access road that had been seized for fifty years, she legally built a waste transfer station and defeated the vicious neighbor who had bullied others for decades.
