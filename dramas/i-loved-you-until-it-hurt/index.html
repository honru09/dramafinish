On their fifth anniversary, Brian Lodge abandons his wife, Linda Grant, and their son, Tim, for his childhood sweetheart. Unable to let Tim lose his father too, Linda gives a begging Brian a second chance… until she uncovers the horrifying truth behind how her son really lost his eye.
