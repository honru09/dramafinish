The hook

Three years undercover, FBI agent Ethan has become the mafia’s second-in-command. When his assigned partner is badly injured, he must pose as the husband of Claire, a feisty butcher passing herself off as a Swiss heiress. Together, they navigate a suspicious mob boss and vicious family power struggles to bring down an international smuggling ring. But as danger closes in, their fake marriage starts to feel dangerously real.
