The hook

Peggy Whitman is the new mom at an elite school, and the PTA has decided she is nobody. When the other mothers target her son and frame him, she is humiliated and pushed aside in front of everyone — a quiet, ordinary mother with no money, no connections and no way to fight back. That is what they see, anyway.

What none of them know is that Peggy is the secret billionaire behind the school itself. Every insult, every setup, every smirk is witnessed by the one woman who can end all of their games with a single decision. She does not reveal herself. She watches. She waits.

And the longer she stays silent, the worse it will be when the truth lands — because once these mothers find out who they framed a child in front of, she is the one who decides their fate.
