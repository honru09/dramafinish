The hook

Peggy Whitman is the new mom at an elite school, and the PTA has decided she is nobody. When the other mothers target her son and frame him, she is humiliated and pushed aside in front of everyone — a quiet, ordinary mother with no money, no connections and no way to fight back. That is what they see, anyway.
