How the Spice score works

Spice is an editorial rating, not a platform content label. We score from the signals a title itself publishes — its genre shelves (mafia romance, dark romance, mates, forbidden love) and the mature themes named in its official synopsis. A contract-marriage CEO romance lands low; a forced-marriage mafia obsession lands at 5. When the signals are weak, we score low or leave the title out entirely.

Selection and scoring rules are recorded with the catalog data. Titles are the platforms' own listings; posters are the platforms' official art.

DramaFinish is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with ReelShort, DramaBox or any other app listed here. Prices and ratings are snapshots (apps: 12 November 2026; pricing: 12 November 2026) and change over time — always check the app before paying.
