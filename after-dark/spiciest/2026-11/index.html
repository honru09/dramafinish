How this issue is built

The pool is our own catalog: every After Dark title with a Spice score, plus every BL/GL title our census confirmed by reading its official synopsis in full. Ranking: Spice first; then desire-signal density (a fixed keyword list counted in title + official synopsis — the same measure as our hero rail); then two disclosed tiebreaks — a bound DramaCPS watch link, and a self-hosted poster — which order equals but never lift a title over a spicier or denser one. Commercial honesty, stated plainly: most Watch buttons here are affiliate links (ReelShort 45% / DramaBox 65% via DramaCPS) and the tiebreak above is the only place that relationship touches the order.

What we did not use: view-count momentum. Our daily view snapshots only began on 12 November 2026, so there is no honest gain to rank by yet — the risers column starts when a full month of readings exists, and we will announce the change here when it does. One pairing-unverifiable title (a ReelShort record whose synopsis we cannot read) is excluded from every board until it can be checked.

Free to quote with a link to this page. Corrections: if a recap, count or rating here is wrong, the title's own page carries its sources — tell us via the About page and we will fix the record, not just the sentence.

Questions about this issue

Are these dramas actually 18+?

They are mainstream app-store titles with mature romantic themes — heavy sexual tension, kissing and adult situations, cut away before anything explicit, because app-store rules cap the content there. Our Spice score rates the heat of the themes and synopses, not explicit content, and this section is gated 18+ because the themes are adult.

How is the Spice score decided?

Spice (1–5) is our editorial rating from the signals a title publishes: its genre shelves and the desire keywords in its title and official synopsis. Monthly ranking then goes Spice first, desire-signal density second, with a bound watch link and a self-hosted poster as disclosed tiebreaks only. The full method is printed on the issue page.
