How to cancel ReelShort

ReelShort charges continue until auto-renew is switched off where you actually paid. Work through the three routes below in order — most people are on the first one.

Step 1: Check your phone's subscription list first

If you subscribed inside the app through Apple or Google, the subscription lives in your store account, not in ReelShort:

  1. iPhone / iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → ReelShort → Cancel Subscription.
  2. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ReelShort → Cancel subscription.

If ReelShort appears there, cancelling there is enough. Your access normally continues until the end of the period you already paid for.

Step 2: If it is not in the list, you were billed directly

ReelShort also sells subscriptions through its own web checkout. Those plans never appear in Apple or Google subscription settings. ReelShort's terms say you can cancel by signing in to your account in the app, opening the VIP page and following the Subscription Management instructions to turn off auto-renewal. Also check PayPal and your card statement for a recurring payment you can stop at the source.

What does not work

  • Deleting the app cancels nothing. The subscription is attached to your store account or your ReelShort account, not to the icon on your phone.
  • Coins are virtual currency: ReelShort's terms treat coin sales as final and non-refundable, so a cancellation stops future charges but does not claw back coin purchases.
  • Keep the cancellation confirmation screen or email. If a charge lands after the date on it, that confirmation is what Apple, Google or your bank will ask for.

Common questions

Does deleting the ReelShort app cancel my subscription?

No. Deleting the app removes it from your phone but leaves the subscription running. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or in the app's VIP subscription page if you paid on the web.

Why is ReelShort not in my iPhone subscriptions?

You most likely subscribed through ReelShort's web checkout rather than inside the app. Web-billed plans do not appear in Apple's subscription list; cancel from the VIP page in your ReelShort account instead.

How much is the ReelShort weekly charge?

The App Store lists a VIP subscription at $19.99 (snapshot 2026-11-12). If your statement shows a different recurring amount, you may be on a web plan or a different tier — check the VIP page in the app.
