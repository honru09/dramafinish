How to cancel DramaBox

One honest warning first: as of our snapshot (12 November 2026), DramaBox does not publish official cancellation instructions. The route that works depends on where the money leaves your account, so identify that first.

Route 1: You subscribed in the app (most people)

  1. iPhone / iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → DramaBox → Cancel Subscription.
  2. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → DramaBox → Cancel subscription.

Access normally continues until the end of the paid period.

Route 2: You subscribed on the DramaBox website

A membership bought on the DramaBox website will not appear in Apple or Google subscription settings — this is the most common reason people believe their membership cannot be cancelled. Sign in with the same account on the DramaBox website or in the app's membership / VIP area and turn off auto-renewal there. Look for the payment in PayPal or on your card statement as well, and stop the recurring payment at that end if the option exists.

If you still cannot find it

  • Deleting the app does not cancel anything — the membership is tied to your account.
  • Use the support contact inside the app and keep a copy of your message and any reply.
  • If charges continue after you have cancelled and support does not resolve it, talk to your bank about the recurring payment, with your cancellation evidence ready.

Common questions

Does deleting DramaBox cancel my membership?

No. Uninstalling never cancels a subscription. Cancel in your store subscription settings, or in your account on the DramaBox website if you paid there.

Why does DramaBox not appear in my subscription settings?

Memberships bought on the DramaBox website are billed outside the app stores, so they do not show up in Apple or Google subscription lists. They have to be cancelled where they were bought.

How much does DramaBox charge per week?

Its cheapest listed membership is $5.99 per week, with higher tiers up to $19.99 (snapshot 2026-11-12). Check which tier your statement amount matches.
