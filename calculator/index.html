Why this matters

Where the prices come from

ReelShort: coin packs and subscriptions from the app's paywall (researched October 2026). 60 coins unlock one episode; the coin total above uses the cheapest pack combination for the episodes chosen, bonus coins counted.

DramaBox: the per-episode coin price is not published anywhere official we could verify, so the coin row shows an estimate range ($0.38–$0.63 per episode) instead of an invented point figure. Subscription prices are the app's published rates.
