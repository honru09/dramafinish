How this chart works

We read each app's own published view count and rank what we find. That makes this a "most watched" chart, not a fastest-rising one — yet. We now store a dated reading every day; once seven days of readings exist, this page switches to weekly gains (▲▼ movement included), the way a chart should work. Until then, no invented movement arrows.
