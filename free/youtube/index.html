Free on YouTube

The apps' own free uploads

The biggest legal free pool for short dramas is not a trick: the platforms themselves post complete series and long compilations on YouTube to promote their apps. Microdramas racked up billions of views there in 2026, and the uploads keep coming.

ReelShort — official channelFull series and compilations from ReelShort itself. Search the channel for the title you want before paying a single coin.
DramaBox — official channelDramaBox's own uploads: complete series and long compilations, free with ads.

How to check a specific title: on any DramaFinish drama page, the watch card links a YouTube search for that exact title on the official channels. If the full series is up there, it costs you nothing but ads; if it is not, the same page gives you the counted free episodes and the cheapest legit route to finish.

We link the official channels rather than re-uploading anything, and we only call an upload official when it sits on the platform's own channel. A per-title index of official uploads is on our roadmap; until it exists, we will not pretend a title is on YouTube when we have not verified it. Uploads titled "full episodes free" on random channels are pirated copies — they vanish, and the mod-APK sites that advertise alongside them are a malware route.
