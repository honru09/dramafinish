The short version

DramaBox is the cheaper app on every published price: its weekly plan costs about a third of ReelShort's, and its annual plan is a quarter of the price. ReelShort is the more transparent app: it publishes exactly what an episode costs in coins, while DramaBox does not. Both make coins the expensive way to finish a long series — on either app, a short subscription beats coin-by-coin unlocking once a series passes roughly 40 episodes (ReelShort) or a handful of episodes (DramaBox, on estimates).
