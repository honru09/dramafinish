About DramaFinish

DramaFinish is a small, independent guide to short drama apps. Its job is simple: show what a series actually costs to finish, where it can be watched legally, and how to stop paying when you are done. It is not a streaming service, it hosts no video, and it is not affiliated with ReelShort, DramaBox or any other app listed here.

Where the data comes from

  • App facts (ratings, rating counts, sellers, versions, release dates): the apps' US App Store listings, collected via Apple's iTunes API. Snapshot: 12 November 2026.
  • Drama catalog (titles, episode counts, view counts, synopses): the public catalogs on the ReelShort and DramaBox websites. Snapshot: 12 November 2026.
  • Prices (coin packs, per-episode coin costs, subscriptions): the apps' published price pages and store listings. Snapshot: 2026-11-12.

Estimates, labelled as estimates

DramaBox does not publish a per-episode coin price. Where we show a DramaBox coin cost, it is a range ($0.38–$0.63 per episode) based on third-party checks of what full series cost in coins, and it is always labelled "estimate". We would rather show a range we can defend than a precise number we invented.

Affiliate disclosure

DramaFinish currently earns nothing from the apps listed here. In future, some outbound links may become affiliate links, which would pay us a commission if you subscribe through them, at no extra cost to you. If that changes, this page will be updated and the links marked. Affiliate relationships will never change a price, rating or verdict published here.

Disclaimer

Prices, catalogs and app features change frequently, and prices differ by country and platform. Every figure on this site carries its snapshot date; treat the app itself as the final word before paying. Nothing here is financial advice — just arithmetic.
